## Design Context

### Users
People sending money home to Nigeria on a schedule — a monthly amount to a
parent, a sibling's school fees, a weekly top-up. Most reach the app inside
MiniPay's in-wallet browser on a mid-range Android phone at 360×640, often on a
slow connection, and many are first-time app users for whom a hex string reads
as an error. The job is: set it up once, trust it, and check it occasionally.
They come back to answer one question — "did it go through?" — and leave.

### Brand Personality
Warm, plain-spoken, dependable. It should feel like a capable friend handling
something important, not a terminal and not a bank. Confidence comes from
clarity (big honest numbers, states that mean what they say), never from
jargon or decoration. Emotions to evoke: reassurance, ease, a little pride in
providing for someone.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm and human. Light theme only — MiniPay is a light in-wallet browser.
Palette: off-white paper tinted faintly toward teal, deep teal-black ink, one
accent of teal for primary actions and key moments, and the existing naira
green (pushed slightly warmer so it never reads as the accent) reserved for
money-received and success states. Typography pairs a
soft, characterful display serif (Fraunces) for headings and hero amounts
with a clean humanist sans (DM Sans) for everything else; tabular figures for
every number. Money-first layouts in the spirit of Wise/Monzo — the amount is
the headline — with interaction patterns Nigerian users already know from
Opay/Moniepoint (bottom actions, verified-account-name confirmation, status
you can read at a glance).

Anti-references: dark-mode fintech with neon accents; crypto dashboards;
anything that puts a wallet address or the word "gas" on screen; card-in-card
layouts; generic rounded-shadow-box templates.

### Design Principles
1. **The amount is the headline.** Every screen leads with the number and the
   person; mechanism comes after.
2. **Say what is actually true.** "Delivered" and "paid out" are different
   things and are never drawn the same. No optimistic states for money.
3. **One thumb, one hand.** Primary actions live at the bottom of the screen;
   tap targets are 44px+; everything works at 360×640.
4. **Warmth through type and tone, not clutter.** Rhythm from spacing and
   hierarchy; decoration only where it carries meaning.
5. **MiniPay's rules are design constraints.** No addresses, no CELO, no
   "gas"; names instead of hex; deposit deeplink instead of a dead-end error.
